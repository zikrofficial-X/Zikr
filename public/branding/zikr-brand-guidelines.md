# ZIKR Brand Identity

The approved visual identity is the supplied gold crescent, Islamic geometric emblem,
hanging lanterns and mosque silhouette on a deep navy field.

## Canonical assets
- `/branding/logo-gold.webp`: primary full brand artwork used by the application Logo component.
- `/branding/og-image.jpg`: canonical 1200x630 social preview image.
- `/icons/icon-192.png` and `/icons/icon-512.png`: PWA installation icons.
- `/icons/icon-512-maskable.png`: dedicated safe-zone maskable icon.

## Palette
- Midnight navy: `#061322`
- Warm gold: `#F5C45C`
- Soft ivory: `#FFF4D8`

Do not use the full detailed artwork as a tiny favicon. Small app icons should use
a centered emblem/crescent crop with generous padding and strong contrast.
